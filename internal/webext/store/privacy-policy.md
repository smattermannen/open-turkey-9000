# Killer Chicken 9000 browser extension: privacy policy

Last changed: 23 November 2026.

The Killer Chicken 9000 extension carries out, inside your browser, the
blocks you set up in the Killer Chicken 9000 program on your computer.

## What the extension reads

To judge a page against your blocks, the extension reads its address, its
title and the text it shows, and the addresses of what it loads (pictures,
videos, frames, downloads).

## Where that goes

Only to the Killer Chicken 9000 program on the same computer, at
`127.0.0.1` (a connection that never leaves the computer):

- which block and list acted on a page, so the program can carry out what
  the block says (for example close the browser, or count it toward bypass
  prevention);
- the sites you use and for how long, every 30 seconds: for allowances,
  and for statistics when you turned them on (the program drops what it
  doesn't need);
- while you record (`kc9k record`): the sites you open, to add them to a
  list, and with words recording, the words of pages, to suggest phrases.

The extension sends nothing to the makers of Killer Chicken 9000 or to
anyone else. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no remote
code. Its settings and the last blocks it received are kept in the
browser's own storage on your computer.

## What the program does with it

The program keeps it on your computer, in its own database, and you can
see and delete it there (statistics can be switched off). If you later
turn on syncing between your own devices, the program, not the extension,
uploads your settings to your own account; it says so before it does.
